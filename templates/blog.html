✨ 1. 5 Hair Care Tips for Healthy & Shiny Hair

Healthy hair is the foundation of a beautiful look. Follow these simple salon-approved tips:

  • Oil your hair at least twice a week to nourish roots.
  • Avoid excessive heat styling like straighteners and curlers.
  • Use sulfate-free and mild shampoos.
  • Trim your hair every 6–8 weeks to avoid split ends.
  • Use conditioner after every wash for smoothness.

🌿 3. Daily Skin Care Routine for Glowing Skin

A proper skincare routine is essential for healthy and glowing skin:

  1. Cleansing – Remove dirt and oil daily
